# Phase 3 interior surfaces brief

- **Status and evidence:** The pre-fix finish disposition was `fix`; the identified hierarchy, rail, H1 ceiling, ordinal, and partner-reachability fixes have landed. `.impeccable/review/desktop.png` and `.impeccable/review/mobile.png` are visually verified full-page captures of `/products/` at desktop and mobile widths. They are evidence for the product-index expression of this system, not homepage captures or exhaustive proof for every related route.
- **Scope and mode:** Phase 3 is a local extension of the approved Evidence-led Industrial Showroom, not a new page comp or visual world. Product, partner, About, and Contact surfaces use Persuade mode with read-like technical hierarchy; Privacy, submission status, and 404 use the same system for focused Read or utility tasks.
- **Shared topology:** Begin with a problem- or status-led interior hero; follow with category-, audience-, evidence-, or policy-specific content; state an explicit publication or operating boundary; then present one clear next action. Do not add a generic section-kicker layer above these headings.
- **Product surfaces:** `/products/` starts from the airflow problem, compares three category starting points, states the unpublished-model boundary, and leads to RFQ preparation. `/products/[category]/` adds a decisive concept-product bay, selection inputs, fit boundaries, and verified-model status. Concept visuals are category-form references only and must remain stamped and captioned until approved product photography replaces them.
- **Partner surfaces:** `/partners/[audience]/` serves distributors, engineering contractors, and equipment manufacturers from the same audience-specific template: first-conversation inputs, coordinated support areas, one real workflow sequence, a scope boundary, and a route to the RFQ workspace. All three guides are reachable from the homepage buyer tabs; only the distributor route is promoted in the shared header.
- **Company and evidence surface:** `/about/` explains supplier and sourcing-partner positioning, supported capabilities, and the evidence still required. Generated storefront, warehouse, product, and nameplate frames remain explicitly labelled placeholders and do not prove the business location, stock, or product record.
- **Contact, privacy, and submission surfaces:** `/contact/` lists pending channel status without live contact links and defines authorization, reachability, and ownership checks. `/privacy/` records that the local RFQ preview has no endpoint, delivery, storage, analytics, upload, retention, or complete rights route. `/thank-you/` is a noindex reserved status route and must not imply that an inquiry was sent. The 404 route offers working product, home, and RFQ paths without inventing an unpublished record.
- **Typography:** Interior H1s use the self-hosted Barlow Condensed display voice, tight leading, and a shared 6rem desktop maximum; the common mobile scale is `clamp(3.35rem, 15vw, 5.2rem)`. Section openings generally use `clamp(2.4rem, 4.1vw, 4.2rem)`, with narrower page-specific variants topping out between 3.5rem and 4rem. The body stack stays at a 1rem floor with 1.5-1.7 line-height. Uppercase microtype is reserved for real concept, contact, or pending-state metadata.
- **Color and material:** Keep warm and cool white fields, deep ink, blue-green structural rules, cool product bays, and scarce safety orange. Component-local light-on-navy, caution, product-bay, and 404 tones are deliberate extensions of the documented palette, not new accents. Flat ruled groups are preferred to glossy cards.
- **Status treatment:** Orange emphasis is horizontal. Hero briefs and notices use a 2px top rule, contained empty or policy status blocks use a 3-4px top rule, and the full-width dark scope boundary uses an 8px top rule. Do not use orange side rails or tabs.
- **Sequence rule:** Decorative ordinals do not appear on unordered categories, capabilities, evidence, checklists, or status groups. Keep numbered markers only for a true workflow whose order changes how the reader uses it.
- **Shared header, breadcrumbs, action, and footer:** The desktop header keeps brand, working destinations, and quote action in a familiar B2B frame; it collapses at 1020px and becomes a compact sticky mobile header at 720px. Breadcrumbs provide ordered slash-separated wayfinding above interior H1s. The final deep-navy ActionPanel has one heading, short explanation, primary orange action, and at most one secondary link, with no eyebrow. The shared footer repeats the product, company, contact, privacy, market-preview, and pending-contact boundaries.
- **Responsive reflow:** Desktop uses broad `max(4.5vw, 32px)` gutters and two-, three-, or four-column layouts according to content. At 1020px, navigation collapses and multi-column grids reduce. At 720px, use 18px gutters and one-column order for heroes, products, fit boundaries, partner/support grids, inquiry preparation, policy/status utilities, action panels, and footer; two-up evidence photography may remain only while labels stay legible. Preserve problem or status first, supporting content second, boundary next, and action last.
- **Unresolved:** Approved real product and business photography; verified public model records and specifications; authorized public contact details; live RFQ endpoint and server validation; privacy consent, delivery, storage, retention, and rights handling. None may be implied by the Phase 3 preview.
