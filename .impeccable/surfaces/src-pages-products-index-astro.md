# Phase 3 interior surfaces brief

- **UI refresh approval and evidence:** The user explicitly approved the homepage and Products desktop/mobile previews and authorized applying their design rules to all existing surfaces. `.impeccable/review/ui-refresh-preview/products-1440.png` and `products-390.png`, their baseline counterparts, `shared.css`, and `products.css` remain the approved reference. The production implementation is complete; desktop/mobile captures for the related routes and current measurement/browser records are saved in `.impeccable/review/ui-refresh-result/`. No repeat approval was required.
- **Historical evidence:** The earlier pre-fix disposition was `fix`, followed by hierarchy, rail, H1-ceiling, ordinal, and partner-reachability fixes. `.impeccable/review/desktop.png` and `.impeccable/review/mobile.png` are earlier `/products/` captures, not current UI-refresh or exhaustive all-route evidence.
- **Scope and mode:** Phase 3 is a local extension of the approved Evidence-led Industrial Showroom, not a new page comp or visual world. Product, partner, About, and Contact surfaces use Persuade mode with read-like technical hierarchy; Privacy, submission status, and 404 use the same system for focused Read or utility tasks.
- **Shared topology:** Begin with a problem- or status-led interior hero; follow with category-, audience-, evidence-, or policy-specific content; state an explicit publication or operating boundary; then present one clear next action. Do not add a generic section-kicker layer above these headings.
- **Product surfaces:** `/products/` uses H1 `Industrial Fan Categories` and section H2 `Choose Your Fan Category`. A wide title row is followed closely by the explanation and RFQ action, then the three category starting points, unpublished-model boundary, and next action. `/products/[category]/` retains its existing category H1 and adds a decisive concept-product bay, selection inputs, fit boundaries, and verified-model status. Detail heroes use two columns at 901px and above with a 380-420px image bay, then stack at 900px and below with a smaller mobile bay. Concept labels, captions, factual copy, and publication boundaries remain intact.
- **Partner surfaces:** `/partners/[audience]/` serves distributors, engineering contractors, and equipment manufacturers from the same audience-specific template: first-conversation inputs, coordinated support areas, one real workflow sequence, a scope boundary, and a route to the RFQ workspace. All three guides are reachable from the homepage buyer tabs; only the distributor route is promoted in the shared header.
- **Company and evidence surface:** `/about/` explains supplier and sourcing-partner positioning, supported capabilities, and the evidence still required. Generated storefront, warehouse, product, and nameplate frames remain explicitly labelled placeholders and do not prove the business location, stock, or product record.
- **Contact, privacy, and submission surfaces:** `/contact/` uses the compact H1 `Prepare Your Fan Inquiry`, lists pending channel status without live contact links, and defines authorization, reachability, and ownership checks. `/privacy/` records that the local RFQ preview has no endpoint, delivery, storage, analytics, upload, retention, or complete rights route. `/thank-you/` is a noindex reserved status route and must not imply that an inquiry was sent. The 404 route offers working product, home, and RFQ paths without inventing an unpublished record.
- **Typography:** Interior H1s retain self-hosted Barlow Condensed, weight 800, and `-0.03em` tracking, with `clamp(44px, 4.5vw, 64px)`, line-height 1, and a wider 18-24ch measure. Mobile H1s use 44px. Compact section headings generally use `clamp(28px, 3vw, 40px)` with 1.08 leading; boundary and action headings may reach 44px. The body stack stays at a 1rem floor with 1.5-1.7 line-height. Uppercase microtype is reserved for real concept, contact, or pending-state metadata. Remaining existing H1 wording is unchanged.
- **Supporting hierarchy details:** Product use-case band H2s use `clamp(24px, 2vw, 28px)`, max-width 74ch, and line-height 1.2. Contact release-check headings have 24px bottom spacing before their tiles. The 404 numeral uses `clamp(10rem, 18vw, 18rem)` on desktop and 9rem on mobile.
- **Color and material:** Keep warm and cool white fields, deep ink, blue-green structural rules, cool product bays, and scarce safety orange. Component-local light-on-navy, caution, product-bay, and 404 tones are deliberate extensions of the documented palette, not new accents. Flat ruled groups are preferred to glossy cards.
- **Status treatment:** Orange emphasis is horizontal. Hero briefs and notices use a 2px top rule, contained empty or policy status blocks use a 3-4px top rule, and the full-width dark scope boundary uses an 8px top rule. Do not use orange side rails or tabs.
- **Sequence rule:** Decorative ordinals do not appear on unordered categories, capabilities, evidence, checklists, or status groups. Keep numbered markers only for a true workflow whose order changes how the reader uses it.
- **Shared header, breadcrumbs, action, and footer:** Align header, content, and footer to a 1440px content measure with desktop gutters `max(32px, calc((100vw - 1440px) / 2))`. The header collapses at 1020px and becomes sticky at 720px. Breadcrumbs remain ordered slash-separated wayfinding with 24px bottom spacing desktop and 20px mobile. Other interior titles and asides align closely rather than occupying distant empty fields. The compact deep-navy ActionPanel keeps one heading, explanation, primary action, and at most one secondary link, with no eyebrow. Footer content boundaries are unchanged.
- **Card interaction and text groups:** Every category card is one native anchor with its heading as accessible name through `aria-labelledby`. Its image, copy, blank area, and decorative footer arrow all share the existing destination, with one focus stop and no nested interactive control. Keep hover/focus border and restrained lift, visible cyan keyboard outline, Enter/modified/middle-click navigation, and reduced-motion behavior. Selection, capability, support, and release-check text tiles follow content height.
- **Responsive reflow and spacing:** Standard section spacing is 40-64px desktop and 28-40px mobile, defaulting to 32px on mobile. At 1020px navigation collapses and multi-column grids reduce; in 721-1020px two-column category grids, center the final card at the same width as preceding cards. At 720px use 18px gutters and one-column order for primary grids, fit boundaries, preparation, utilities, action panels, and footer; two-up evidence photography may remain while labels stay readable. Preserve problem/status first, supporting content second, boundary next, and action last.
